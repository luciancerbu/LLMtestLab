### Visual & World Requirements:
1. **Arena:** Roughly **40 × 40** unit play space. Ground is a flat `StaticBody3D` plane (or thin box) at **y = 0**, material albedo `#2a9d8f` (teal grass vibe). Subtle grid or edge trim optional.
2. **Sky & lighting:** `WorldEnvironment` with a soft sky gradient (top `#87CEEB`, horizon `#e9c46a`) or ProceduralSkyMaterial equivalent. **DirectionalLight3D** (warm sun, slight angle) + low-intensity fill so shadows read on obstacles. Optional very light fog (density ~0.002) for depth — keep it subtle.
3. **Obstacles (all procedural — no GLB imports):** Place **10–14** distinct obstacles using `MeshInstance3D` + matching `StaticBody3D` collision on the same transform. Mix these types:
   - **Step blocks** — `BoxMesh` 2×1×2, stacked into short staircases (2–4 steps high)
   - **Tall crates** — `BoxMesh` 1.5×3×1.5, color `#e76f51`
   - **Wide platforms** — `BoxMesh` 4×0.5×3, elevated 2–6 units, color `#457b9d`
   - **Pillars** — `CylinderMesh` radius 0.6, height 2–4, color `#4a4e69` (jump-on top must be flat and reachable)
   - **Goal platform** — elevated `BoxMesh` 3×0.5×3 at the far end of the arena, material albedo `#55ff33`, emission strength **1.5** (glows — this is the win zone)
4. **Color palette (material albedo):** ground `#2a9d8f`, steps `#f4a261`, crates `#e76f51`, platforms `#457b9d`, pillars `#4a4e69`, goal `#55ff33`. Use `StandardMaterial3D` with roughness 0.7–0.9 (stylized matte, not shiny plastic).
5. **Player body:** `CharacterBody3D` with collision only — **no visible player mesh** in first person (hide or omit body `MeshInstance3D` so the camera view is unobstructed). Optional: simple `MeshInstance3D` on a separate editor-only layer for debugging, disabled at runtime.
6. **Decor (minimal):** 3–5 small `SphereMesh` "orbs" (radius 0.2, emissive `#ffd60a`) placed on top of some obstacles as optional collectibles — purely visual or simple pickup counter; do not block core movement if skipped.

### Player Controller (CharacterBody3D — core):
Implement in `player.gd` attached to the player node.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Move speed | 6.0 units/s |
| Sprint multiplier | 1.5× (hold Shift) |
| Jump velocity | 7.5 |
| Gravity | 20.0 |
| Coyote time | 0.12 s (grace jump after leaving ground) |
| Jump buffer | 0.10 s (early jump input before landing) |

**Movement rules:**
- WASD moves relative to **camera yaw** (use horizontal look direction only — flatten Y to zero, normalize). Do not rotate a visible body mesh; the camera rig carries yaw.
- `move_and_slide()` every frame; floor snap enabled.
- Jump only when on floor OR within coyote window; respect jump buffer.
- Player capsule collision: `CapsuleShape3D` radius **0.4**, height **1.2**; origin at feet (collision center y = 0.6).

**Coding traps (common failures):**
- Obstacle mesh and `StaticBody3D` collision **must share the same transform** — misaligned collision lets the player fall through or float above surfaces.
- Jump must use `velocity.y = jump_velocity`, not `position.y +=` — teleport jumps feel wrong and break collision.
- Camera-relative movement must zero out look direction Y before normalizing, or WASD will push the player into the ground when looking down.
- First-person pitch must apply to the **camera pivot only**, not the entire `CharacterBody3D` — otherwise horizontal movement breaks when looking up/down.

### First-Person Camera:
- `Camera3D` as child of a **Head** node (or `Neck` pivot) on the player, positioned at eye height **y = 1.6** above the player's feet.
- On game start: capture mouse with `Input.mouse_mode = MOUSE_MODE_CAPTURED`. **Esc** releases capture; click to re-capture.
- Mouse look (or arrow keys fallback): horizontal rotation on the player body (yaw); vertical pitch on the Head pivot only, clamped **-85° to +85°**.
- Default FOV: **75°**. Press **V** to toggle between normal (75°) and wider "showcase" FOV (90°) for demo footage.
- Optional subtle head bob when moving (±0.03 units vertical sine wave) — keep it mild so it does not cause motion sickness on video.
- No third-person orbit camera — stay first person throughout.

### Goal & Reset:
- Attach an `Area3D` (box shape ~3 × 2 × 3) to the goal platform. When the player's body enters → status `REACHED GOAL`, freeze player input, show banner **"Goal reached!"**
- Press **R** to reset player to spawn `(0, 1, -15)` facing +Z, clear goal state, reset camera yaw/pitch.
- Kill plane: if player **y < -5** → teleport to spawn (no game over screen needed).

### HUD (`CanvasLayer`):

- Speed readout (units/s)
- Optional: `Orbs: 2/5` if collectibles implemented
- Hint line: `WASD move · Space jump · Shift sprint · Mouse look · V FOV · R reset · Esc release mouse`
